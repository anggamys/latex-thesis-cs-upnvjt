\section{Research Limitations}
\TemplateTodo{Write limitations honestly and specifically, for example data, time, tools, methods, validation, or user coverage limitations.}

This study has several limitations. First, the dataset is limited to one application (GoFood), so generalization to other applications needs further testing. Second, the classification only covers two classes (positive and negative) without including a neutral class. Third, the web scraping method only collects public reviews and may not represent the entire user population.
