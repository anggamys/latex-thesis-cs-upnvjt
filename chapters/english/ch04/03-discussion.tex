Based on Table~\ref{tab:comparison}, the Na\"ive Bayes Classifier shows better performance than SVM across all evaluation metrics. NBC accuracy reached 86.3\%, 4.2\% higher than SVM. This indicates that NBC is suitable for text classification with independent features.

The NBC recall of 88.1\% indicates that the model is effective at detecting positive sentiment. The lower precision (84.7\%) suggests that some negative reviews were incorrectly classified as positive. This is common in review data containing sarcasm or ambiguous language \cite{gewang2022}.

These results align with studies by Go et al. \cite{go2019} and Gede et al. \cite{gewang2022}, which reported that NBC provides competitive results for text-based sentiment classification.
