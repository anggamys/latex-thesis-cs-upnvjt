\subsection{Preprocessing Stage}

Before classification, the review text goes through four preprocessing stages:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Case folding}: converting all text to lowercase.
  \item \textbf{Tokenization}: splitting sentences into constituent words.
  \item \textbf{Filtering}: removing stopwords, punctuation, and numbers.
  \item \textbf{Stemming}: converting affixed words to base form using the Nazief-Adriani algorithm.
\end{enumerate}
