\subsection{Na\"ive Bayes Classifier}

The Na\"ive Bayes Classifier is a probabilistic classification method based on Bayes' Theorem with the assumption of feature independence \cite{han2012}. Although the independence assumption is rarely satisfied in the real world, NBC has proven effective in text classification. Bayes' Theorem is expressed as:

\begin{equation}
P(C \mid X) = \frac{P(X \mid C) \cdot P(C)}{P(X)}
\end{equation}

where $P(C \mid X)$ is the probability of document $X$ belonging to class $C$, $P(X \mid C)$ is the probability of document $X$ occurring in class $C$, $P(C)$ is the prior probability of class $C$, and $P(X)$ is the probability of document $X$ occurring.
