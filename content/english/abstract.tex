\noindent The growth of online transportation and food delivery service users in Indonesia generates a massive volume of reviews on platforms such as the Google Play Store. These reviews contain valuable user opinions, yet they are difficult to analyze manually. This study aims to classify the sentiment of GoFood application reviews into positive and negative classes using the Na\"ive Bayes Classifier (NBC) method. The dataset consists of 5,000 Indonesian-language reviews collected through web scraping. The preprocessing stage includes case folding, tokenization, filtering, and stemming. Features are extracted using TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency). Evaluation using 10-fold cross validation shows that NBC achieves an accuracy of 86.3\%, precision of 84.7\%, recall of 88.1\%, and F1-score of 86.4\%. These results indicate that NBC can effectively classify review sentiment and can serve as a reference for further sentiment analysis system development.
